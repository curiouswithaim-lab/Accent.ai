# Human-Centered UX Copywriting & De-AI-ification Skill

This skill guides the rewriting of user interface text, system labels, and analytical summaries to make them sound like they were authored by thoughtful, seasoned human practitioners—not generated by an LLM with a thesaurus.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Human UX Writing

1. **Clarity Over Grandiosity**: Say what things actually are. Real professionals do not call a file upload an "Autonomous Ingestion Vector" or a report a "Forensic Dossier".
2. **Eliminate AI "Buzzword Density"**:
   - 🚫 **Ban list**: *delve, testament, pivotal, courtroom-ready, biostatistical synthesis, forensic terminal, telemetry replay, unprecedented, multifaceted, tapestry, robust, seamless, synergy, scientific manipulation*.
   - ✅ **Preferred terms**: *audit, check, finding, satellite observation, filed claim, field alert, difference, verified, baseline, rainfall, wind direction, forest canopy*.
3. **Write Like an Inspector or Auditor**:
   - Tone is calm, authoritative, factual, and direct.
   - Use active voice: "Satellites detected 96 mm of rain during sampling" instead of "Autonomous telemetry corroboration manifests severe monsoon dilution".
4. **Respect the Reader's Cognitive Load**:
   - Shorter sentences.
   - Plain English explanations of complex geospatial math.
   - Distinct, informative labels on numbers and charts.

## 2. Transformation Reference (Robotic AI ➔ Natural Human)

| Robotic AI Pattern | Why It Fails | Natural Human Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| *Forensic Regulatory Terminal* | Pompous, sounds like a movie hacker | *Independent Satellite Verification for Environmental Filings* |
| *Biostatistical Synthesis Review* | Overcomplicated AI jargon | *Expert AI Audit Review* or *Audit Analysis* |
| *Curated filings with known spatio-temporal confounding* | High cognitive friction | *Sample filings demonstrating common baseline manipulation techniques* |
| *Autonomous Classification: Classified as...* | Announces the machine's inner thought | *Document Type: EIA Clearance Filing* |
| *Filed Claim & Satellite Reality in Middle Explaining in Plain English* | Clunky instructional phrasing | *Claim vs Reality* or *What was Filed vs What Satellites Saw* |
| *Ground-action dispatch event publisher* | Robotic, overly abstracted | *Field Inspector & Registry Alerts* |
| *Courtroom-ready findings* | Melodramatic | *Defensible, verified findings backed by satellite imagery* |

## 3. Human UX Copy Review Checklist

When auditing verbatim copy in an application:
- [ ] Are headings clear, self-explanatory, and free of hype?
- [ ] Do tooltips and subtitles explain *why* the user cares, rather than describing internal backend classes?
- [ ] Do status banners explain real-world consequences (e.g., "Inspection alert sent to field team", "Credits placed on hold")?
- [ ] Are numbers given intuitive context (e.g., "8× above normal river flow" rather than "800% hydraulic confounding vector")?
- [ ] Are buttons actionable verbs ("Run Audit", "View Inspector Alert", "Download Sample")?
